# recovery-day

1. Use health only when the owner shares it: the shared health context or get_health. If health is not shared, missing or stale, it says nothing about the owner; do not guess how they feel and do not ask them to share. The owner's own words about how they feel count as much as any reading.
2. Judge whether today asks for a lighter plan. Read query_calendar for what is fixed and what can give. Speak about the body in plain words (running low, a short night, recovering well), never a score, a number or a reading, and do not repeat health to anyone else or in a message for someone else.
3. Offer at most 3 small changes as choices: ease or move something that can give (propose_calendar_change, for their approval), protect sleep tonight, keep the day's anchor. This is not medical advice: do not diagnose, name a condition or advise on medicine or dosing. If the owner describes symptoms that sound serious, suggest they speak to a doctor or local emergency services.
