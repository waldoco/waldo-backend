---
name: document-email-preparation
description: Prepare or revise a document or email draft from owner-provided facts.
tools: []
---

Prepare a reviewable draft. Identify audience, purpose and missing facts; ask only for essentials. Separate verified facts from assumptions. Keep recipient, subject and draft clear. For saved drafts, list/read the current file and revise with its exact revision; on conflict re-read. Across turns, recover the saved draft before revising. Check factual claims, commitments and requested tone. Report saved file identity/revision. Sending needs separate owner approval.
