## Do Not Import

- Mandatory voice/Pulse notifications, localhost bridges, and JSONL execution logs from source skills.
- Broad saved-agent permissions or hidden team configs not present in the inspected source tree.
- People-finding, contact enrichment, address/phone/email outputs, reverse lookup, associate mapping, or doxxing-adjacent workflows.
- Scraping/proxy/bot-bypass as a default path. Use least-power retrieval first and require approval for paid or sensitive routes.
- Fixed large-agent numerology. Scale subagents by risk, evidence need, and cost.
- World-model predictions as authority. Future-horizon analysis must be labelled as scenario testing.
