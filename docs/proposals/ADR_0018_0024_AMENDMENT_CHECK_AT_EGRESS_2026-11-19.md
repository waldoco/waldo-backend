## The principle

**The model sees what the owner can see. Deterministic checks run where data leaves (a send, a draft, a memory write, a log or trace, a query to a third party), where money moves, or where something cannot be undone.**

This is the repository's own rule (`CLAUDE.md`): deterministic rejection only for auth, secrets, canaries, owner checks, egress, schema validation and medication dosing. Every other "should Waldo read this, may Waldo do this" is the model's judgment, given the full context. It matches how Claude Duet and Meta Muse place the defence: the model reads freely, and a separate check outside the model screens outgoing actions.

Three consequences:

1. **Taint is a label on data, not a verdict on a turn.** A tool result that other people could have written is still stamped external (`withGoogle` at `tools/live/google.ts:80`, `read_memory` at `tools/live/memory.ts:47`). The stamp still rides the turn (`conversation/tool-loop.ts:481-482`). What changes is what the stamp blocks. It blocks effects that leave the owner's space or cannot be undone. It does not block the owner's own words or the owner's own undoable space.
2. **Provenance is checked on the span that carries it.** An owner correction is decided by where its quote comes from (the owner's current message), not by what else the turn read. A secret is redacted where it sits, not by hiding the message it sits in.
3. **Real boundaries stay hard and unchanged:** sends and replies to other people, payments and purchases, deletes and other irreversible effects, secrets and credentials, health in sends, drafts and memory, and medication dosing. Nothing here weakens a send, payment, delete or secret check.

The threat model is prompt injection: text written by someone other than the owner (mail, an invite, a web page, a connector answer, a note learned from those) steering Waldo. Injection is dangerous where it makes data leave, money move or something vanish. Inside the owner's own undoable space, the defence is the receipt and the undo, not a card.

## Consequences

- Corrections stick after any lookup, and the always-loaded block shows what the owner last said.
- Inward, undoable writes the owner asks for run after a calendar or mail read, with a receipt and an undo, as 13 Nov D1 ruled.
- What outside content can still do, and how each is caught: steer a send (recipient guard and card, unchanged), a delete (card, unchanged), a payment or credential entry (card, unchanged), persistent automation (`set_standing_order` card, unchanged), a widening of authority (refused, unchanged), health into memory (refused, unchanged). It can still steer an inward, undoable write; the reply lists it and the owner can undo it.
