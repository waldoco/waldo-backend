# Personal agent experience research for Waldo

Verified 2026-09-20. Scope: Instinct, Grok Bot, Meta Muse, Vellum Assistant, Poke, Folk personal AI. Sources are first-party product pages, official docs and an official engineering article. No product was personally exercised. Observed facts below mean that the cited page explicitly makes the claim; they are not independent reliability measurements. Confidence is high in attribution, unknown in operational success rates. No user-provided hands-on Instinct success story exists.

## Recommendation

[inference] Build one personal assistant that remembers corrected understanding, owns a small set of active commitments, wakes from schedules/events, takes permitted actions, and returns with either verified progress or one meaningful decision. Browser execution supports that loop; a permanently running desktop and unrestricted computer control are not prerequisites for the smallest winning experience.

The strongest initial loop for Waldo is a health-aware day that adapts: learn priorities and energy, read permitted calendar/health context, propose a usable day, make narrowly authorized calendar adjustments, carry one follow-up to an exact send approval, watch for a response, and revisit the plan when facts change. This is a proposed product choice derived from the user's desired outcome, not a competitor capability claim.

## Architecture practices worth transferring

1. **The durable agent is not the computer.** [inference] Persist goals, current commitments, memory, approvals, schedules and results outside a disposable browser/session. A browser can crash or expire without losing what Waldo owes the user. Meta documents durable Postgres application state outside the untrusted runtime, with separate credentials and action approval services. [Muse engineering](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse)
2. **Action authority is separate from generated prose.** [inference] A practical MVP needs server-enforced allow/ask/deny at the action boundary, exact approved inputs and single-use approval handling. It does not need a second frontier agent inspecting every harmless thought. Muse routes concrete action requests through an independent authority; Grok recommends narrow scope and warns that model-based review complements least privilege. [Muse engineering](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse), [Grok approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy)
3. **Proactivity must have a durable trigger and a reason to interrupt.** Vellum distinguishes one-shot and recurring jobs, periodic checks, provider watchers, and cheap notification/script execution. [Scheduling](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/key-concepts/scheduling) [inference] Waldo can start with one schedule/event mechanism, a last-seen cursor, deduplication and quiet-hours-aware delivery. Do not invoke the full agent to send a deterministic reminder or poll unchanged data.
4. **Corrected facts must win.** [inference] Store provenance, current value, supersession and deletion separately from chat summarization. A remembered preference never creates an action permission. Vellum explicitly describes correction replacing stale understanding and bounded retrieval; use those properties, not necessarily its wiki implementation. [Memory](https://www.vellum.ai/docs/key-concepts/memory-and-context)
5. **Reusable skills are procedures, not an excuse for unbounded autonomy.** [inference] Package validated procedures with inputs, tools, failure paths and result checks; schedule only after a successful one-off path. Grok's skill/routine guidance makes that sequence explicit. [Skills/routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)
6. **Keep interruption, approval and results legible.** [inference] The initial app should expose chat, today's plan, current work, a small decisions inbox and memory correction. This is enough to make background work comprehensible; a graph of agents and a permissions console at onboarding are not required. Muse's design account supports activity/goal visibility and structured approvals. [Design](https://introducing.muse.ai/)

## Smallest complete winning demonstration

Proposed acceptance scenario, not a statement that it currently works:

- User shares two priorities, calendar access, optional health access or self-reported energy, quiet hours and a narrow rule allowing changes to Waldo-created focus blocks.
- Waldo prepares the day and explains one adjustment using source timestamps, without presenting a health diagnosis.
- With the laptop off, a calendar change arrives; Waldo replans within the existing rule and leaves an action receipt.
- It remembers an investor follow-up commitment, prepares the exact recipient/subject/body, obtains exact send approval and records the provider message ID.
- It detects the reply or reaches the agreed follow-up time; the next useful step is taken or proposed once, without duplicate sends or noise.
- The user corrects a preference; the next plan uses the corrected value. Disconnecting a provider or revoking a rule stops subsequent affected action.
- The same responsibility later becomes visible in Telegram or an eligible WhatsApp route without creating a second Waldo. A Kennel task can be represented now, while implementation of its full execution bridge follows later.

## Claims to reject or qualify

- No basis to call Waldo's life/work combination unique: Folk documents coding-agent delegation, Grok documents bot coordination, and Poke lists development integrations.
- No evidence that health-aware assistance itself is unique: Poke advertises Oura, Folk advertises habit/fitness use cases, and Muse describes training plans. Waldo must win through reliable integration of optional health context into priorities and action, demonstrated by users.
- No competitor parity claim is justified by a checklist or documentation audit. Evaluate identical approved tasks and failure cases before claiming it.
- Vendor plan prices do not establish Waldo's cost; they may bundle/subsidize compute or impose usage limits. Measure its model tokens, wake-ups, browser minutes, provider calls and support burden per successful loop.
- Instinct internals remain uncertain. Vellum hosting-default descriptions conflict. Public product claims were not operationally verified. No paid retrieval, authenticated account access or external posting was used.
