## What this PR builds

Every item is merged dark: its flag is `"0"` in production and staging. A claim becomes true for testers only once the gate does the steps in its row.

| Claim | Commit | Flag | True for testers once the gate | Honest wording until then |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Swaps the hard workout | Swap a workout for an easier one in its slot | `HEALTH_EASE_WORKOUT` | 1. Redeploys `connector-proxy` from this branch. 2. Sees a judged h1/p10 pass with the flag on. 3. Turns the flag on at a named staging deploy. | "After a bad night it protects your sleep and moves or eases the hard parts of your day, with your yes." |
| Every change undoes | Undo a calendar cancel; the app counts undo down | `CALENDAR_UNDO_CANCEL`. `undo_until` is not flagged and fills on the next deploy for existing create and move undo, within the app contract. | 1. Redeploys `connector-proxy`. 2. Turns the flag on. Afterwards, every calendar change Waldo makes (add, move, rename, cancel) undoes in one tap for 10 minutes. Gmail drafts, Google Tasks and local writes (reminders, memory) still don't. | "Changes Waldo makes to your calendar undo in one tap for 10 minutes. Anything that goes to another person waits for your yes, because once sent it can't be taken back." |

**Handles prescriptions, ruled by the gate.** Medicines are special-category health data, so they go on the Supabase health plane, not DO SQLite. The ADR-0081 amendment is proposed in Pin4sf/waldo-brain#53, and the owner rules on it. The backend build comes after that, in its own PR, behind `MEDICATIONS="0"` with migration `20261101040000`. It isn't true before the amendment is accepted and that PR is merged, migrated and switched on, so it won't be true by 2 Nov.
- **Interim wording that is true today:** "Reminds you to take your medicines at the times you set. It never advises on doses." Generic recurring reminders already do this (`contracts/src/tools/schemas/reminders.ts:9-14`). Taken or missed tracking and reorder nudges are not built yet.

**Not built, and why:**
- **Gmail draft undo and a generic local undo:** each needs a new provider method or a new app route, and neither fits before 2 Nov with review.
- **Models and Telegram:** these are wording and the owner's choice. See the table above.
